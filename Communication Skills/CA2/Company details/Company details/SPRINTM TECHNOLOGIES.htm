Job Profile

About the Role:
We are looking for energetic and self-driven individuals to join SprintM Technologies as Business Development Trainee. This is a target-driven role, where you will be responsible for business growth, sales, and client relationship management. We need achievers—people who thrive in challenges, set goals, and consistently achieve them.

Internship Details:
** Duration: 5 Months
** Location: Hyderabad
** Type: Full-time (Internship + Pre-Placement Offer)
** Stipend: Rs.27,000 per month (Rs.15,000 Fixed + Rs.12,000 Incentives)
** Training Period: First 10 days are unpaid training
** Conversion to Full-Time: After successful completion, interns will be directly offered full-time employment

Compensation (Post Internship):
** CTC Rs.6 LPA (Rs.4 LPA Fixed + Rs.2 LPA Incentives).

Role Expectations:
We are looking for candidates who:
** Are goal-oriented and target-driven – ready to take ownership and achieve targets
** Can take responsibility for any assigned tasks
** Are individual contributors with a strong sense of accountability
** Can work in a collaborative and supportive environment
** We need Extroverted and excel at building relationships
** Have a creative and innovative mindset
** Joining Date: Immediate – Candidates must be ready to join within 7-10 days after the selection process.
** Work Culture: 6-day office (Sunday off), 11 AM - 8 PM

Why Join SprintM Technologies?
** No pressure – Work in a supportive and encouraging environment
** Friendly work culture – We believe in teamwork and collaboration
** Hands-on learning – Gain deep insights into the business field
** Be a core team member – Work closely with the founders and contribute to company growth
** Exciting workplace – Enjoy birthday celebrations, office parties, and daily cash prizes for top performers
** Work-life balance – 6-day workweek (Sunday off), office hours from 11 00 AM – 8 00 PM
