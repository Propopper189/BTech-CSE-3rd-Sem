About Company

SagaciousElevate is the Intellectual Property (IP) Research and Analytics Business Unit of Elevate, formed following the integration of Sagacious IP into the Elevate organization. As part of Elevate, SagaciousElevate is committed to providing efficient and high-quality IP support to innovators, businesses, investors, and patent professionals worldwide. Our services span a wide range of technical domains including Biotech, Pharma, Mechanical, Electronics, and Telecom. With a strong track record of delivering impactful patent research and analytics, SagaciousElevate continues to work with a diverse clientele—from Fortune 500 companies and global law firms to start-ups and academic institutions. Our team comprises professionals from top-tier institutes such as IITs, NITs, and BITS, contributing to a dynamic and innovation-driven work environment.
