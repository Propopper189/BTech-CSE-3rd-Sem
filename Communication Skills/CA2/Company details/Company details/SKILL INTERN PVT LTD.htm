Job Profile

1. RESPONSIBILITIES – BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT ASSOCIATE
As a Business Development Associate at Skill Intern Pvt. Ltd., you will play a pivotal role in driving the growth of our Ed Tech platform by identifying and engaging potential clients, building strong relationships, and contributing to the overall business strategy.

Your key responsibilities will include:
** Gaining an in-depth understanding of all products and services offered by Skill Intern.
** Developing and executing strategic sales plans to meet organizational targets and drive revenue growth.
** Identifying new business opportunities through market research, lead generation, cold calling, networking, and outreach campaigns.
** Acquiring new clients through direct engagement, referrals, and coordination with the marketing team.
** Building and maintaining strong, long-term relationships with existing clients to foster
trust and ensure client retention.
** Recommending relevant product upgrades and additional services tailored to client needs.
** Collecting and analyzing client feedback to improve service quality and client satisfaction.

2. RESPONSIBILITIES- SALES SPECIALIST
** Generate and qualify leads through calls, emails, and online platforms
** Conduct product demos and explain course features to prospective students
** Understand customer needs and recommend suitable programs
** Follow up with leads and close enrollments within timelines
** Achieve monthly and quarterly sales targets
** Maintain accurate lead and sales data in CRM

3. RESPONSIBILITIES- SALES SPECIALIST
** Conduct product demonstrations and explain course features to potential students and parents
** Present the benefits and outcomes of the EdTech programs effectively
** Engage with leads generated by the sales and marketing team
** Understand customer requirements and tailor demos accordingly
** Follow up with interested leads to convert them into enrollments
** Maintain detailed records of demo sessions and feedback in CRM
** Collaborate with the sales team to achieve monthly targets
** Participate in webinars, workshops, and online sessions to promote programs
** Provide insights to the marketing team based on customer interactions
** Ensure a professional and positive experience during every demo session

REQUIREMENTS:
** Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
** Strong interest in sales, marketing, and business development.
** Ability to identify leads, build relationships, and convert prospects.
** Ability to work independently and as part of a team.
** Self-motivated with a results-driven attitude, Strong problem-solving and negotiation skills & Good time management and organizational abilities. Willingness to learn and adapt in a dynamic environment.
** Freshers and candidates with relevant internship or experience are encouraged to apply.
